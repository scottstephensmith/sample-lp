# Getting Started with Static Sites

Static sites are making a comeback, and for good reason. They're fast, secure, and simple to maintain.

## Why Static Sites?

1. **Speed**: No database queries or server-side processing
2. **Security**: Fewer attack vectors
3. **Simplicity**: Easy to develop and deploy
4. **Cost**: Cheaper hosting options

## How to Begin

1. Choose your tools
2. Set up your development environment
3. Create your content
4. Deploy your site
